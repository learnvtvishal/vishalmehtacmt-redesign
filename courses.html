Questions

Frequently asked.

What is the refund policy?

If a paid course is not right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of purchase. You get a full refund as long as you have watched less than 20% of the course. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Which devices can I learn on?

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. Your progress is saved to your account, so you can start a lesson on the laptop and finish it on your phone.

What happens when my access period ends?

You will get WhatsApp and email reminders before it ends. After expiry the paid lessons lock again, but your progress, quiz scores, receipts and certificates stay in your account. You can renew at any time.

How do I get WhatsApp and email updates?

Just sign up with your mobile number and email. You will automatically receive a welcome message, payment confirmations, live-class reminders and progress nudges. You can opt out from any message.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Finish every lesson and pass the short quiz at the end of a course (2 out of 3 correct) and a verifiable certificate of completion is issued in your name.

Are there any stock tips or calls in the courses?

No. The courses teach process, rules, risk and review. There are no tips, calls or recommendations to buy or sell anything.
