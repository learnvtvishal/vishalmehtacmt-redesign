Why these three numbers

Risk first, entries second.

Most traders spend their time on entries. Professionals spend it on risk. If you decide in advance that one losing trade will cost, say, 1% of your capital, the position size is no longer a feeling; it falls straight out of the distance between your entry and your stop-loss.

The risk-reward calculator shows the other side of the same coin: a plan that risks ₹10 to make ₹20 only needs to be right about one time in three to break even before costs. And the drawdown table explains why both matter. A 50% loss needs a 100% gain just to get back to where you started, so keeping losses small is what keeps you in the game.

For a full walk-through with a worked example, read Position Sizing: The One Rule That Keeps You in the Game.
