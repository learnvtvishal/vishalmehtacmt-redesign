In 60 minutes

What you'll learn.

Purely educational. No tips, no calls, no "secret indicator". Just the framework disciplined traders use to stay in the game.

01

Write your rules down

Why a trade without written entry, exit and invalidation rules is a guess, and how to turn your own idea into a one-page rulebook.

02

Fix your risk per trade

The 1% idea, how position size follows from your stop-loss, and why sizing matters more than any single entry.

03

Journal like a professional

A simple trade log that shows you which mistakes cost the most, so you review behaviour, not just profit and loss.

04

Think in systems

How rules, risk and review connect into a repeatable process, and how to test it honestly before real money touches it.

Is this for you?

Who this is for.

  • Trade part-time and keep breaking your own plan
  • Have had a few good months wiped out by one bad week
  • Want a process you can follow calmly, alongside a job or business
  • Are curious about systematic or algo trading but want the basics first
Not a fit if you want
  • Stock tips, calls or "sure-shot" trades
  • Promises of fixed or guaranteed returns
  • A shortcut that skips risk management
